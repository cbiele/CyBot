# Cybot STL reconstruction — assembled model, print list, motor pockets

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Inputs:** `Cults3d_v5/` (68 STLs), `4FingersGripper/` (6 STLs), `URDF/` link meshes,
`Cybot assembly 1.6.pdf`.
**Outputs:** `model/cybot-master.blend` (the master assembly, open in Blender 5.1; rebuilt by
`apply_placements.py` from `model/placements.json`), `model/bearings.json`,
`model/subassemblies/<folder>.blend` (exploded per-folder files), `model/transforms.json`,
`model/parts-list.md`, renders `model/pass3-*.png` and `model/closeup-*.png`.
Everything is regenerated by the scripts in `tools/blender/` (run with
`/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b -P <script>`).

## 1. What the files turned out to contain

- **Assembly coordinates survive in the STLs.** The base (`2-axis1`), main body plates,
  axis-2 yokes, and the elbow holder (`axis4_axis5_holder`) are exported in one global
  frame in millimetres. The URDF link meshes are the same frame in metres and are the
  fully assembled links, so they serve as the ground truth.
- **Each joint subassembly sits in its own local frame** centred on the joint: the
  axis-2 and axis-3 cycloid stacks, the axis-4 group, the wrist group (`6-axis5-6`,
  offset by −460 mm in z), and the gripper.
- **One file = one placed instance.** The three axis-3 disks are byte-identical files;
  shafts, OPH pairs and housing pairs are positioned instances. The print quantity is
  therefore the file count, with exceptions: `axis5-holderNoSplit` is an
  *alternative* to `axis5-holder` + `axis5-holder2`; the three `beltTentioner`
  parts carry no placement at all (they sit at the origin); `plateBall` is one
  file but four feet; and the gripper's `grip`, `Axe` and `T8_to_Axes` are one
  file each but four fingers.
- **The URDF predates the gripper** (2022 vs 2023), so the gripper was placed
  analytically: identity rotation, adapter face on the axis-6 flange at x = 288.5 mm.

## 2. Registration

Local groups were fitted to the URDF with trimmed ICP from a grid of starting poses
(`tools/blender/register_groups.py`, `fix_axis4_gripper.py`). Median residuals are
about 5 mm because the URDF meshes include motors and bearings the printed parts do
not, and because the URDF is a 2022 revision of a 2024 STL set. Use the model for
layout and clearance between *printed parts*, which are exact within a group, and
treat cross-group positions as ±5 mm.

| Group | Rotation | Translation (mm) | Median | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| axis2 cycloid | ~identity | (0.8, −2.6, 204.2) | 5.2 | joint centre z ≈ 204 |
| axis3 cycloid | 180° about z | (−0.6, −3.4, 452.9) | 4.9 | joint centre z ≈ 453; 90th percentile 43 mm, pulley side uncertain |
| axis4 group | 90° about z | (90.4, −0.8, 586.6) | 6.2 | tube along +x; residual tilt, ±10 mm |
| wrist | ~identity | (−1.4, 0.0, 460.0) | 4.9 | pure z shift |
| gripper | identity | (380.5, 13.8, 554.0) | — | analytic |

## 3. Print list and bed fit

Full table with dimensions and volumes: `model/parts-list.md`. Totals: 74 files, of
which 73 print (one alternative). Solid volume 6.8 litres, roughly 4 kg of filament at
40 % infill.

**Bed (P2S, 256 mm cube):** every part fits as exported except the two main-body plates
(380 × 142 × 16 mm). Both are split by `tools/blender/split_plates.py` into
`model/split/mainBody{1,2}_{lower,upper}.stl`.

**Joint (decided 2026-09-06).** No straight cut exists between z 256 and 388 that misses
every feature: the Ø20 bolt pockets occupy z 250–279 and the motor and encoder
pockets take over from 275. So the split is a **40 mm half-lap** from z 258 to 298:
the lower piece keeps the *outer* half of the 16 mm thickness in that band, the upper
piece keeps the *inner* half. Every existing bolt pocket in the band (the three Ø20
pockets and the first motor bolt) then clamps the two laps together, and two extra
M4 clearance holes at x ±55, z 268 pin the corners. Glue the lap. Pieces: lower
166 mm, upper 254 mm, both flat on the bed. Plate 1's original STL has nine
non-manifold edges near its top; the split script closes them, so the split pieces
are cleaner than the source. The 170 mm base parts and the 209 mm wrist holder fit
without splitting.

## 4. Motor and encoder map, from the geometry

Detected by bolt pattern on the mesh (`tools/blender/mount_patterns.py`,
`wrist_slots.py`): NEMA17 = four holes or slots on 31.0 mm, NEMA23 = four holes on
47.14 mm, Omron E6B2 = three M3 on a 30 mm pitch circle with a 22 mm pilot.

| Axis | Motor | Where | Evidence | Free length behind the mounting face |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NEMA17** | top of `axis1-stepperHolder`, shaft down through a Ø32 bore into the belt cavity | 31 mm square of M3, Ø32 pilot | open |
| 1 enc | E6B2 | top of `axis1-encoderHolder` | 3× M3 on Ø30, Ø22 pilot | open |
| 2 | **NEMA23** | `mainBody1` inner face, 69 mm square pocket 5 mm deep, Ø40 pilot; body between the plates | 47.14 mm square of Ø4.9 | **61.0 mm** to `mainBody2` |
| 3 | **NEMA23** | same, second pocket | same | **61.0 mm** |
| 2/3 enc | E6B2 ×2 | `mainBody2`, 42 mm square pocket 6.5 mm deep, Ø22 pilot | 3× M3 on Ø30 | 62.5 mm |
| 4 | NEMA17 | slotted plate on `axis4_housingNema`, motor axis z ≈ 647, body toward the wrist | 4 slots 13 × 3.4 on 31 mm | open |
| 5 | NEMA17 | outer −y face of the wrist holder, slots at z ≈ 540 | 2+2 slots on 31 mm | open |
| 6 | NEMA17 | outer +y face of the wrist holder, slots at z ≈ 571 | 2+2 slots on 31 mm | open |
| gripper | NEMA17 | `mainHolder` −x face, body toward the wrist | 31 mm square, Ø30 bore | **55.0 mm** to `OPH_To_Gripper` |

**Discrepancy with the PDF.** The guide says three NEMA23 and four NEMA17. The v5
geometry has **two NEMA23 (axes 2, 3) and five NEMA17 (axes 1, 4, 5, 6, gripper)**. The
base holder has a 31 mm pattern and a Ø32 bore, which a NEMA23's Ø38.1 pilot cannot
pass. Trust the geometry.

## 5. MKS smart-stepper fit

MKS boards mount on the motor's rear face and need an exposed rear shaft for the
magnet. Added depth is not published in a datasheet I could reach; product listings
put the SERVO57D bundle at 76 mm overall on a 56 mm motor, so plan on **15–20 mm**
and measure the first unit.

| Axis | Space | Motor + board | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2, 3 (NEMA23) | 61.0 mm | 41 mm motor + 15–20 = 56–61 | **Marginal.** Either a 41 mm dual-shaft NEMA23 with the board measured under 18 mm, or recess `mainBody2`'s inner face by 6–8 mm at (x 26.6, z 310) and (x −24.2, z 356). The recess must stop short of the encoder pockets, which start 0.6 mm and 2.8 mm away in z. A through-window is possible but weakens the plate. |
| 1, 4, 5, 6 (NEMA17) | open | 47 + 15 | Fits. Only the arm's own swing envelope matters; check axis 4's motor against the elbow holder at the extremes of joint 3. |
| gripper (NEMA17) | 55.0 mm | 47 + 15 = 62 | **Does not fit.** Use a 34–40 mm pancake NEMA17 (34 + 15 = 49), or lengthen the printed `OPH_To_Gripper` adapter by 10 mm. |
| encoders | 62.5 mm | E6B2 body 40 | Fits, unchanged. |

## 6. Known gaps

- Joint stacks and pulleys were re-seated by hand after the fit (2026-09-06):
  housings centred in the plate gap at the Ø75 hole centres, motor-side pulley
  against plate 1's outer face, encoder-side pulley against plate 2's. The
  Fusion export had the pulleys pulled outboard, which a rigid group fit cannot
  undo.

- Belt tensioner placement (three parts) is not encoded; place from the PDF page 12
  photo when the body is assembled.
- Pulley sides were assigned by rule (motor side = plate 1, encoder side = plate 2),
  not by the fit; confirm against the PDF page 14 photo during assembly.
- Axis-4 group: the housing, motor plate, tube and 80T pulley files are dated
  Dec 2021 to Jan 2022 and were exported from their own part files, not the
  assembly, unlike the disks, output plates and shafts (Oct 2022). They were
  seated by rule (2026-09-06): rotation snapped to exactly 90° about z, gearbox
  axis on the elbow holder's Ø85 end bore at (y 0, z 555), output-plate boss at
  x 84-105 per the URDF link_4 cross-section, housing x 48-73 inside the holder,
  disks phased 180°, input pulley coaxial at x 27-46, tube from x 105.
- Axis-5 gearbox parts (housing halves, 80T pulley, rotor, pulley, spacer) were
  exported at the wrist group's origin and have been moved by rule to the wrist
  pitch joint at (251.5, 0, 535.7); confirm on assembly.
- Cross-group clearances involving the wrist in motion were not swept.

## 7. Bearings and exploded subassemblies (2026-09-06)

Bearing seats were inferred from circular bores on planar faces of the placed
STLs, matched to the PDF hardware lists. Results live in `model/bearings.json`
(one instance per bearing: type, dimensions, centre, axis, the two parts it
couples). `tools/blender/explode_group.py -- <folder>` builds
`model/subassemblies/<folder>.blend` with two collections, the true assembly
(hidden) and the exploded stack, bearings floated above the part they seat in,
and writes `<folder>-assembly.md` with the stack order and hardware.

**Axis 2 (1:20), stack along y, joint centre (0.3, y, 203.1):**

| Bearing | Qty | Seat | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6813zz 65×85×10 | 2 | Ø85 step 5 mm deep in each housing half; Ø65 boss 10 mm long on each OPH | y −33..−23 and +22.5..+32.5 |
| 6804zz 20×32×7 | 5 | Ø32 centre bore of each disk on a Ø20 eccentric journal (2 mm throw); Ø32 bore 11 mm deep in each OPH on the Ø20 shaft ends | disks y ≈ +11, 0, −11; OPHs y ≈ −26, +25 |
| 688rs 8×16×5 | 18 | six Ø16 pockets per disk on a Ø57 pitch circle, Ø13 through behind, pockets open toward −y | one set per disk |

**Disks re-seated (`tools/blender/seat_axis2_disks.py`).** The Fusion export had
the three disks up to 4 mm off the shaft eccentrics and their pocket patterns
turned 31° from the Ø8 rod holes in the holders and pulleys, which sit at
0°/60°/… about the joint axis. Each disk is now rotated so its pockets sit on
the rod angles and translated onto the eccentric section of the shaft it rides
(disk3 on shaft4/3 at (−0.7, 201.3), disk2 on shaft3/2 at (−1.1, 204.7), disk1
on shaft2/1 at (1.8, 203.0); joint axis (0.0, 203.0)). `axis2-diskCap` is one
per disk (three instances): a 3.5 mm ring with a Ø35.6 counterbore for the
6804's 2.5 mm protrusion and six Ø16 counterbores for the 688s' 1.5 mm
protrusion, seated on the floor of each disk's 4.5 mm deep Ø84 recess on the
stepper side. Assembly per disk: drop the 6804 in the bore and six 688s in the
pockets from the recess side, then the cap.

The 688 bore is 8 mm and the PDF specifies M4 threaded rod (3.85 mm) cut to
length, so the rods roll inside the 688s with 4 mm clearance = twice the 2 mm
eccentricity; the same Ø57 circle carries six Ø8 holes in both holders and both
pulleys, so the rods tie pulley, holder and disks together. Each housing half
has twelve Ø6 holes for the M6×50 ring pins (the PDF says 14 for 1:20; the STL
has 12). The 6813 outer ring is split 5 mm in the housing and 5 mm in the
main-body plate's Ø85 pocket (y ±28..33). Inferred, not proven: the pocket side
of the 688s and the nut stack on the M4 rods.
